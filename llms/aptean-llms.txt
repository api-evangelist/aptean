# Aptean

> Aptean is a global provider of industry-specific, AI-powered enterprise software (ERP, CRM, and supply chain) for manufacturing, distribution, food & beverage, and other complex industries. Its developer-facing surface is the Aptean Integration Platform (AIP) — an event/webhook platform that lets products publish events and lets consumer tenants subscribe and receive them.

## APIs

- [Aptean Integration Platform API](https://stg.integration-graph.apteansharedservices.com/swagger/index.html): Event-driven integration API — producers, consumers, event definitions, events, and public keys. Bearer JWT + APIM subscription headers.
